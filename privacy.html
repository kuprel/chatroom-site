ChatRoom Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

ChatRoom is a peer-to-peer video chat app that renders you as a live 3D avatar of your real face. This policy describes exactly what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data goes directly to the people in your room, and nowhere else.

Face data

ChatRoom uses the front (TrueDepth) camera to track your face and build a texture of it. This face data — imagery, geometry, and expression coefficients — is used for exactly one purpose: rendering your avatar to the people in your chat room.

  • It is transmitted directly to the devices of people in your room. It is never sent to our servers, because our servers cannot receive media at all.
  • A face capture is stored on your own device so a headset you own can display it. You can delete it at any time with “Delete Saved Face Data” in the room menu.
  • Face data is never used for identification, advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than drawing your avatar, and is never shared with third parties.

Voice audio

Your microphone audio is compressed and sent directly to the people in your room. It is not recorded or stored anywhere.

Head pose and movement

Head position, rotation, and facial expression coefficients are sent to room peers to animate your avatar. They are not stored.

What our server sees

A small signaling server helps devices in a room find each other. It sees your IP address and a random per-session peer number — that is all. It never receives faces, audio, video, or names. There are no accounts, and we collect no analytics.

Data retention and deletion

The only persistent data is the face capture stored on your own devices and your local settings (such as blocked peers). Deleting the app, or using “Delete Saved Face Data,” removes the face capture. Nothing about you is retained anywhere else, because nothing about you is collected anywhere else.

Children

ChatRoom is not directed at children and is rated for users 17 and older.
