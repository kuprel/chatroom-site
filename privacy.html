3D Chat Room Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes rooms, optional AI conversations, and safety tools. The updated app uses Sign in with Apple for human rooms and requires a private invitation. Eve is an AI companion with a one-time five-minute preview without an account. Sign in with Apple to continue after the preview. Rooms last up to one hour per session. The preview and one-hour limit take effect with the next app update.

Accounts and private invitations

The updated app uses Sign in with Apple and requests your name, without requesting your email. When Apple provides your name, the app saves only your first name and last initial on this device for the profile button. This name is not sent to our services or other participants. You can edit it in Account. Apple authenticates you and provides an app-specific identifier and authorization code. Our Google Apps Script service exchanges the code directly with Apple, validates the response and stores the identifier and a refresh token to keep you signed in and detect revoked access. Tokens are never shared with room members. The app stores its session credential in the device-only Keychain. Sessions last up to 90 days of inactivity and are renewed during use; Apple access is checked at most once per day and the app checks Apple's credential state on return. Members see a stable member label. Installation identifiers link to the account to enforce blocks and bans across devices. Existing email accounts can migrate on an authenticated device; their stored email is removed when migration succeeds. Earlier app releases retain their expiring email-code sign-in for compatibility.

Private invitations are unguessable codes shared by the host, expire after one hour, and can be revoked. Membership records and connection details expire with the room within 24 hours; active connection details are discarded after inactivity. Rooms admit at most four verified accounts. There is no public room directory.

Use Account → Delete Account in the lobby to revoke Apple sign-in access and delete your profile and sign-in sessions and close rooms you host. We retain minimal pseudonymous identifier links needed to resolve safety reports and enforce bans. Recreating a banned account does not remove its ban. Contact us for privacy requests or an appeal.

Face data — collection

With your consent and camera permission, 3D Chat Room uses ARKit face tracking and the front camera, including the TrueDepth camera on supported devices. It processes camera images of your face, a three-dimensional face mesh, facial-expression blend-shape coefficients, head position and rotation, and eye positions and rotations. It derives a face texture, face-shape corrections and an average skin color for the avatar. The app does not access Face ID authentication templates or use your face to identify or authenticate you.

Face data — purposes

Face data is used only to draw and animate your live 3D avatar, including its appearance, expressions, eyes and head movement, and to adjust the viewpoint of the 3D scene. You can see your own avatar in the mirror view. There are no other planned uses. We do not use face data for advertising, analytics, profiling, identification, or training AI or machine-learning models.

Face data — sharing and disclosure

In rooms, expression coefficients, head and eye poses, and view parameters are sent to participants you allow to animate built-in avatar artwork. Camera imagery, captured face textures, custom face-shape corrections and face meshes are not transmitted to other human participants. Face imagery can be used locally in your own mirror view.

If you explicitly connect an iPhone to the Mac companion, face data is sent to that Mac for rendering. The current companion applies the same room restrictions. Face data is not sent to the developer's account/moderation service, Google, xAI, advertising services or analytics services. Face data is not included in safety reports. In Eve's room, your face data is rendered locally.

Room packets use encryption with pair-specific keys provided by the membership service. Other participants can separately capture what their devices display or play; the app cannot control their screenshots or recordings.

Face data — storage, retention and deletion

The iOS app does not save captured face data to files, databases, backups, or cloud storage. It processes face data in volatile CPU and GPU memory on your device and receiving participants' devices. Leaving a room stops camera capture and transmission; working buffers, including your most recent face texture, can remain in the running app's memory until replaced or the app process ends. Closing the app process releases that memory. There is no developer-held face-data record to delete. You can stop further capture by leaving the room or revoking camera access in iOS Settings.

Voice audio and optional AI conversations

In rooms with friends, live microphone audio is encrypted and sent directly between the participants who allow each other. Friend audio is not uploaded to Google or xAI, transcribed, classified by an AI service, or saved as recordings by the app or developer. Devices temporarily buffer audio in memory for live playback. Use the microphone button to mute yourself; Hide, Report, Block or leaving the room stops that participant's audio and further sharing.

The Google-hosted service handles sign-in, invitations, membership, connection information and safety-report metadata; it does not relay friend audio. Automatic voice transcription and content classification are not used in friend rooms. New participants remain hidden and muted until both people allow each other.

If you choose an AI room and accept the separate AI voice disclosure, your microphone audio, conversation text and game context (such as chess moves and game results) are sent to xAI to generate the AI companion's replies. The initial five-minute preview does not collect an email address. A random, device-only Keychain identifier identifies the preview. The Google-hosted service stores a hash of this identifier, elapsed preview time and an active-session identifier to enforce the one-time allowance across reconnects. It issues short-lived xAI connection tokens; the provider's master key stays on the service. Face data is not sent to xAI. The app holds audio and conversation text in memory and does not save a conversation history to disk. xAI's published API policy describes default retention of inputs and outputs for 30 days for abuse auditing, after which they are deleted; it says API data is not used for training without explicit permission. We do not authorize training on your conversations. See xAI API data practices and xAI's privacy policy. Declining AI companion sharing leaves rooms with friends available without sending their audio to xAI. You can withdraw AI voice consent at any time with Stop Sharing AI Voice with xAI in Account; no further audio or conversation text is sent to xAI unless you agree again.

Connection data and device identifiers

The Google-hosted membership service receives room membership, installation identifiers, network addresses, port numbers and connection information so invited members can connect. Active clients refresh membership every three seconds and stop exchanging media when membership cannot be refreshed for 20 seconds. The service does not relay friend audio or avatar animation. Cloudflare's STUN service receives your network address and port to help establish direct connections; it does not receive room media. There is no paid media relay in this release, so some restrictive networks may prevent a connection.

The installation identifier is random, not derived from your face and not an advertising identifier. It accompanies reports and is linked to your verified account for safety enforcement. Google and Cloudflare may maintain operational request logs under their own policies.

Safety reports and block notifications

Reporting content or blocking a participant sends the action, reported participant's and reporting installation's identifiers, room, time, app version, selected reason, and any description you enter to the developer. No face imagery, face-tracking measurements, automatically generated transcript or audio recording is attached. Please do not include private information in the description.

Reports are sent over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script operated by the developer and emailed to brkuprel@gmail.com for review. Google processes report metadata to host the receiver and deliver email. Unsent notification payloads are stored in the script's private storage for up to 30 days and removed after successful notification. A separate private review record contains the report ID, reported installation ID, room, reason, receipt time, 24-hour deadline, decision and review note. Open review records remain until resolved; resolved records are deleted within 30 days, or sooner when storage space is needed. Limited report identifiers, content hashes and delivery timestamps are retained for up to 30 days to prevent duplicate notifications. Delivery may be delayed by service outages or email limits. Notification emails are retained until the safety review and any related follow-up are complete, then deleted, unless retention is required by law. Pending reports are stored on your device and retried until delivery is confirmed; the local pending copy is then removed. Deleting the app removes unsent reports. Contact the developer to request deletion of a submitted report. See Google's privacy policy for its service data practices.

Moderation enforcement

The app fetches current moderation policy over HTTPS on entering a human room and every minute while there. The policy exposes only banned installation identifiers and whether rooms are enabled; it does not expose reports, reporter identifiers, allegations or review notes. Google receives the network request, but the app does not include your installation ID in this policy request. Banned account and installation identifiers are kept for as long as the ban is in force so clients can remove content and reject further contact, including after reinstall or account recreation.

Participant approvals and temporary hides last for the current room visit. New participants' content is withheld until you explicitly allow it. Human rooms use built-in avatar artwork instead of transmitting captured images and direct encrypted voice chat without cloud transcription. Reports include the description you provide and do not upload audio or face media.

Local settings and your choices

Your device stores terms and privacy consent, AI voice consent, the random installation identifier, blocked identifiers, appearance settings, and unsent safety reports. Your shortened Apple sign-in name, any name you edit in Account and an optional photo you choose are stored on this device for your Apple account. They are not sent to our services or other participants; deleting your account removes them from this device. Its device-only Keychain stores sign-in credentials and the random Eve preview identifier. These are separate from face data. You can undo blocks, withdraw AI voice consent, sign out, or delete your account from Account in the lobby. Deleting the app removes its local app settings; Keychain items may remain on the device, so reinstalling does not grant another preview; restoring a device backup may restore local settings. To request deletion of developer-held report data, contact the address below. No advertising or cross-app tracking is used.

Earlier builds

Earlier invitation-room builds uploaded short voice messages through Google to xAI for transcription and safety checking. That upload route is disabled with this update, so those builds must update to use friend voice chat. Audio previously sent to a provider remains subject to that provider's retention policy; disabling this route does not erase previously submitted data.

Builds before 148 used public rooms and could send face textures and custom face geometry directly to other participants. Those builds did not have verified accounts, private invitations or pre-delivery voice checks. The September 25 privacy policy remains available for those builds. The older face-data handling and safety-report retention apply to activity that occurred in them.

Children

3D Chat Room is intended for people aged 18 and older.
