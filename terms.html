3D Chat Room Terms of Use (EULA)

Last updated: September 17, 2026

You must explicitly agree to these terms before accessing a room or starting face tracking. No account registration or login is required. You must be at least 17 years old to use 3D Chat Room.

Acceptable use

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not transmit pornography or sexually explicit content, harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, exploitation of minors, illegal content, spam or impersonation. Respect other participants and stop any interaction they do not want.

Reporting and blocking

Open Participants in a room, or tap a participant's avatar, to access safety controls. Choose Report Content, select a reason, optionally describe what happened, and tap Send Report to flag objectionable content to the developer.

Block & Notify Developer immediately removes the participant's avatar and audio from your view and stops sending your media to that participant. Blocking also sends an inappropriate-behavior report to the developer. If delivery fails, the report is saved on your device, its unsent status is displayed, and it is retried automatically. A block continues to apply while the notification is pending. Local blocks persist across app launches and can be removed from the lobby.

Report metadata contains participant identifiers, the room, time, reason and any description you supply. No face imagery or audio recording is attached. Please give enough detail for the developer to investigate.

Enforcement

The developer reviews safety reports and may exclude participants who violate these terms from room discovery. Reports are allegations and are reviewed before service-wide action is taken. Report urgent safety concerns to brkuprel@gmail.com. Live calls are not recorded by the app; you can leave any room immediately.

Your content and privacy

Your avatar imagery, expression and movement data, and voice are transmitted to participants in your room to support live conversation. Only share what you intend those participants to see and hear. AI voice chat separately requires your agreement to send audio and conversation text to xAI. See the Privacy Policy for collection, use, sharing, storage, retention and deletion details.
