3D Chat Room

3D rooms for friends on iPhone, with built-in avatars, chess, Connect Four, and short voice messages checked for safety before delivery. Eve is a separate, optional AI companion.

The updated app is for adults 18 and older. Sign in with Apple to chat with friends by invitation. Each room lasts one hour and admits up to four people. There is no public room directory or random matching. Try Eve for five minutes without an account, then sign in with Apple to continue. No subscription is required.

Help and safety

  • Sign in: tap Sign in with Apple under Chat with friends. Your signed-in status and Sign Out appear on the main page.
  • Invite a friend: create a room, open Participants and tap Invite Friends to send the link in Messages. Your friend taps the link to join. If the app is not installed, the link opens the App Store. After installing, tap the invitation again. Each person must allow the other participant before receiving their content.
  • Remove content: use Remove Message Immediately for a voice message, or Hide Content Immediately for a participant.
  • Report or block: open Participants & Safety and choose Report Message, Report Content, or Block & Notify Developer. Objectionable content and abusive behavior are prohibited.
  • Delete your account: leave the room and choose Account → Delete Account on the main page. Minimal safety identifiers may be retained for abuse investigations and enforcement, as described in the Privacy Policy.

For support or to report an incident, choose Report. Include a description of the incident and the approximate time, plus the in-app report ID if available.

Direct connections depend on both participants' networks. If a room cannot connect, try a different Wi-Fi or cellular network. The app requires iOS 26 or later.
