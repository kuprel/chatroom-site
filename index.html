3D Chat Room

Private 3D rooms for friends on iPhone, with built-in avatars, chess, Connect Four, and short voice messages checked for safety before delivery. Eve is a separate, optional AI companion.

The private-room release, version 1.0 (build 148), is for adults 18 and older. Human rooms require verified email accounts and a private invitation. There is no public room directory or random matching.

Help and safety

  • Sign in: request an email code in Private rooms with friends, then enter the six-digit code within 10 minutes.
  • Invite a friend: create a private room, open Participants and copy the invitation. Your friend can paste it into Join by Invitation. Each person must allow the other participant before receiving their content.
  • Remove content: use Remove Message Immediately for a voice message, or Hide Content Immediately for a participant.
  • Report or block: open Participants & Safety and choose Report Message, Report Content, or Block & Notify Developer. Objectionable content and abusive behavior are prohibited.
  • Delete your account: leave the room and choose Delete Account in the private-room lobby. Minimal safety identifiers may be retained for abuse investigations and enforcement, as described in the Privacy Policy.

For support or to report inappropriate activity, email brkuprel@gmail.com. Include a description of the incident and the approximate time, plus the in-app report ID if available.

Direct connections depend on both participants' networks. If a room cannot connect, try a different Wi-Fi or cellular network. The app requires iOS 26 or later.
